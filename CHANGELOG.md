# Release notes

## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-21

Version archived for the IEEE JSTARS submission.

### Contents

- `code/` — 20 scripts: assigner instrumentation, four-stage trace with identity
  validation, similarity substitution, static-proxy audit, soft-label and score
  analysis, anchor-ownership analysis, diagnostic-subset construction, image-level
  bootstrap, subset-robustness comparison, case selection and case figure
  rendering, table and figure generation.
- `config/p13_cases.csv` — the nine cases in Fig.~6, with the rank quantity that
  selected each one.
- `code/test_overlap_arbitration.py` — unit test showing that a contested anchor is
  assigned by localization overlap rather than by the alignment score. It fails if a
  future version of the library changes the arbitration rule.
- `code/run_eval_ema60.sh` and `code/make_eval_ema60_table.py` — re-evaluation of the
  existing epoch-60 EMA checkpoints under the official protocol, so that the
  assignment statistics and the detection metrics can be compared on one weight node.
  No training is involved.
- `config/` — dataset descriptor, fully expanded training arguments for both arms,
  image identifier lists of both diagnostic subsets, the sampling manifest and
  balance report of subset B, and the per-run resume counts.
- `results/` — four aggregate JSONs: subset-robustness comparison, image-level
  bootstrap for both subsets, static-proxy seed/subset summary.
- `manifest.csv` — one row per training run: seed, resumption, best epoch,
  diagnostic checkpoint hash, assigner parameters.
- `data/README.md` — how to obtain AI-TODv2, convert it, and rebuild the
  training-visible annotation map. No imagery is redistributed.
- `tools/make_archive.sh` — assembles the Zenodo payload, which adds the 32
  per-ground-truth trace files that are too large for the repository.

### What this version does not contain

- Trained weights. They are identified by SHA-256 in `manifest.csv` and are about
  0.5 GB in total; the training arguments plus the hash are sufficient to re-create
  them.
- AI-TODv2 imagery, which remains under its original licence.
- The prediction files used for the fixed-threshold diagnostic (about 95 MB per
  arm). They are regenerated by the evaluation script; the derived per-GT traces
  are included.

### Reproducibility checks performed at this version

1. All seven supplementary tables regenerate byte-identically from the archived
   traces (`make_supp_tables.py`).
2. The three identities S2=S3pre, S3post=S4 and S3pre−lost+gained=S3post hold on
   every image of both subsets; the trace aborts otherwise.
3. The static-proxy arm asserts S1 equality between its two arms on all 75,065
   boxes of the full annotation and on all 26,273 boxes of the two subsets
   (12,184 in A, 14,089 in B).
4. The static arm returns an identical rate for all four seeds within a subset,
   which is the check that the substitution does not leak weights.
